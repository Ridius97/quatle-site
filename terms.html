5. Reports and moderation

  • You can report a player and block them from their profile or a chat. Blocking works at once.
  • A report is a signal, not proof. A person (a moderator) reviews it and decides. We never punish because of one report alone, and the reported player is not told who reported them.
  • Automatic protective pause: if three different, independent players report the same player for harassment or threats within 72 hours, that player's chat is paused for up to 24 hours while a moderator reviews the reports. This pause is not a penalty and is lifted if the reports are dismissed.
  • When a moderator confirms a violation, the measure usually follows this order: a warning, a 3-day chat restriction, a 7-day suspension, a permanent ban. Older violations stop counting after 90 days. Serious violations (threats, hate, sexual content, sharing personal information) can lead directly to a 7-day suspension or a ban; anything that endangers a minor or is illegal leads to a permanent ban and, where the law requires it, a report to the authorities.
  • You will receive a notice that says what the measure is, until when, which rule it is about, that a moderator decided it, and how to appeal.
  • After you report a player, you receive a short notice that your report was reviewed. To protect everyone's privacy we do not share the outcome.
  • Reporting players on purpose without reason is misuse. Repeated unfounded reports can lead to a warning, and then your reports no longer trigger the automatic pause.

6. Appeals

  • You can appeal each decision once, within 6 months. A moderator reviews it again.
  • Warnings and chat restrictions: appeal in the app, in Settings → Privacy & Safety → Moderation decisions.
  • Suspensions and bans: you cannot sign in, so e-mail quatle.support@gmail.com from your account's e-mail address and explain why you think the decision is wrong.
