Report illegal content

Quatle, the online multiplayer quiz game. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Who can use this

Anyone, also without a Quatle account, can tell us about content on Quatle that they believe is illegal, for example a username, a suggested question or a message (EU Digital Services Act, article 16).

Players can also use Report on the player's profile or in the chat, which is faster.

How to report

Send an e-mail to quatle.support@gmail.com with the subject Illegal content report and include:

  1. Why you believe the content is illegal (which law or right, if you know).
  2. Where it is: the username of the player, and where you saw it (profile, chat, question) with the date.
  3. Your name and e-mail address. You may leave out your name if the report is about child sexual abuse material.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the information in your report is correct and complete.

Please do not send screenshots that contain other people's personal data unless they are needed to show the problem.

What happens next

  • We confirm that we received your report.
  • A person reviews it, carefully and without unnecessary delay, and decides whether the content breaks the law or our Terms of Service.
  • We tell you our decision and how you can challenge it.
  • If the information suggests a threat to someone's life or safety, we inform the competent authorities.

Emergencies

If someone is in immediate danger, call the emergency number 112 first.
