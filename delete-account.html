Delete your Quatle account

Quatle, the online multiplayer quiz game. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Before you start

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. Active battles end as a loss for you and your opponent wins.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Quatle and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and type DELETE to confirm.
  4. Tap Delete my account. Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: by e-mail (without the app)

Use this if you no longer have the app or cannot sign in.

  1. Send an e-mail to quatle.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Quatle account.
  2. Send it from the e-mail address of your Quatle account and write your username. We only delete an account when the request comes from its own e-mail address.
  3. We delete the account and reply to you, usually within a few days and at the latest within one month.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, level, XP, wins and losses.
  • Your e-mail address, password, date of birth, settings and notifications.
  • Friends, friend requests and blocked players.
  • Your chats and messages (also for the other player).
  • Your battles (also from your opponents' lists).

What is kept for a while, and why

  • Safety records (reports and moderation decisions) are kept without your name or e-mail for up to 24 months, to protect other players and to handle legal claims.
  • Your username stays reserved for 90 days, so nobody else can take it and pretend to be you.
  • Only if your account is suspended or banned: a protected (hashed) form of your e-mail address is kept until the suspension ends, or for 24 months after a ban, so the same e-mail address cannot sign up again during that time.
  • Questions you suggested that were approved stay in the game without your name.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.
